## About Me

<img class="profile-picture" src="ame.jpeg">

Welcome to my site. I'm a Ph.D. student at the Department of Statistical Sciences of the University of Padua, Veneto, Italy. 

I'm currently working on Graphical Models for Multivariate Extremes, under the supervision of professor Carlo Gaetan and professor Alberto Roverato. My short CV is available [here](https://amerigonovaro.github.io).

## Research Interest

My research focuses on statistical methodology, in particular on graphical models and multivariate extreme value theory. In the past, I have also worked on Functional Data Analysis and Bayesian modeling for mortality data.
